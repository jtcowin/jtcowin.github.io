<Section id="context" title="Context" kicker="01">

Phi Labs shifted from Archway to Bolt Liquidity four months after I joined. The new protocol required a clear product story, consistent technical explanations, audience-specific messaging, and a process for keeping claims accurate as the product evolved.

</Section>

<Section id="foundation" title="Marketing foundation" kicker="02">

I synthesized interviews with leadership, engineering, business development, pricing, sales, and foundation stakeholders into a 16-document operating foundation. It covered product positioning, buyer roles, messaging architecture, market pain points, competitors, claims governance, audience psychology, voice, terminology, reusable copy, and category narrative.

That foundation is internal working material, so it is described here rather than published.

</Section>

<Section id="documentation" title="Technical documentation" kicker="03">

I drafted all 27 documentation pages as MDX files and pushed updates through GitHub. Engineering built the code sections, and I collaborated with design on the documentation’s visual branding.

The architecture page is the clearest example of the translation work: market data, oracle, smart contracts, and settlement explained as one sequence rather than four disconnected concepts.
