<Section id="context" title="Context" kicker="01">

Bolt Liquidity was still in beta, had limited product visibility, and operated without a paid marketing budget. The creator program needed to generate credible, native content while building relationships with creators and complementary Web3 brands.

</Section>

<Section id="role" title="My role" kicker="02">

I managed the programs from initial concept through distribution. That included creator selection and relationships, housing and travel logistics, contracts with our legal team, sponsor outreach, package negotiation, production planning, scripting, directing, shot and draft reviews, publishing, tagging, timing, and coordinated engagement.

</Section>

<Section id="sponsorships" title="Sponsorships" kicker="03">

Trezor, Bitrefill, and Rayls Labs purchased co-sponsor packages tied to the activations. I negotiated content deliverables and timelines, and managed communication between each sponsor, the creators, internal stakeholders, and production collaborators.

</Section>
